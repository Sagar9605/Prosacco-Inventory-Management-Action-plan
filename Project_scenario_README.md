Project scenario:

In this assignment, I have created a comprehensive inventory plan for a fictitious supply chain operation (Prosacco) that handles 100 unique items. 
To achieve this goal, I had to first identify and address missing data, determine inventory coverage, calculate safety stock levels, estimate demand data, 
evaluate warehouse capacity, compare shipping costs, and determine target revenue. This assignment helped me gain the practical skills and knowledge needed
to make informed decisions about inventory management and supply chain efficiency; optimizing resource allocation; and minimizing costs while maintaining service levels.

Objective: 

The purpose of this assignment is to generate a comprehensive inventory plan for Prosacco’s supply chain operation. 

The file contains 4 sheets: 

1) The SALES & CURR INV sheet contains the demand information where I have performed my calculations. 
2) The LOGISTICS DATA sheet contains the cost and lead time of different movements from every possible origin and destination combination. 
3) The WH DATA sheet contains the dimensions required to calculate the WH space. 
4) ACTION PLAN. This is the space I have used to develop my inventory plan.
